# Short reservation emails: Marwane's answers on the open points

Date: 2026-11-23 (#406)

- **The venue zone ("Paris time") is named once, on the event time.** The
  free-cancellation deadline on the next lines is in the same zone and does not
  repeat it. Why: twice read as clutter; this keeps the October 20 rule.
- **Cancellation, postponement and refund emails drop the street address** and
  keep the date, time, venue name and city. Why: there is nothing left to attend.
- **The subject carries the evening's date** ("Your place is booked for
  Thursday, December 3") instead of the brand name, which the sender already shows.
- **The button stays "Manage or cancel my reservation"** rather than "Open my
  reservation", so the guest sees where to cancel.
- **The paperclip that the inline logo adds to emails without a QR is accepted**
  (see `2026-11-20-reservation-email-logo-inline.md`).
- **EN and ES copy is reviewed during the real mailbox test**, not before.
