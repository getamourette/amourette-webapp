# Booking form: no place count, deposit terms as two rows

Date: 2026-11-23 (#405)

- **The booking form no longer shows how many places are left.** Marwane's call
  after seeing the before/after. A full evening still switches to the waitlist;
  only the number is hidden.
- **The deposit terms are two short rows, not a paragraph:** the deposit and its
  refund on arrival, then the free-cancellation deadline. Why: guests have to
  read both before paying, and a bold lead per row is read at a glance where a
  four-line sentence was skipped. After the deadline only the deposit row stays,
  followed by the late-cancellation acknowledgement.
