References and comparison method

B is The fleeting tie. C is The first exchange. Both are loaded directly from round 10. The lettering comes directly from the two round 9 SVGs, including the reconstructed R and the revised R/E spacing.

The same SVG boxes and placements are used for both candidates. Their original relative scale is retained; C is intentionally not enlarged to imitate B's outline. Switching to “Wordmark only” hides the companion mark but reserves its space, so the rest of the layout stays still. Phone icons remain visible.

The welcome screen retains the existing landing copy, Figtree and Jost typography, wine glow and red identity treatment. The QR copy and compositions are proposals. “More presence” is the initial viewing setting, not an approved weight.

These emblems came from generated studies fitted to vector contours. They remain exploratory, with no final selection, contour refinement, or app integration. Read the full method and asset provenance.
