What changed, and what stays open

The original round 10 SVG stays exactly as it was. B1 reconstructs the three ribbon shapes with fewer, deliberately placed Bézier curves. It smooths the left loop's upper and lower transitions, cleans the tiny point at the top-right loop's return, and evens the crossing and loose ends. This is a new interpretation of the contours, not a claim that their geometry is unchanged.

B2 is the free alternative. It uses B1's loop geometry with shorter loose ends and a slightly wider gap above the right end. Compare whether its compactness helps or takes away some of the original movement.

The wordmark is loaded unchanged from round 9. Choosing the ribbon direction does not select either new drawing or a final wordmark weight. No micro-size master, production logo, or application integration is approved by this page.
