Original assets, proportions and what remains open

B1 is loaded from the selected ribbon SVG. Both Balanced and More presence reuse their original round 9 contours. Each complete source is moved and scaled uniformly; no letter, pair spacing or ribbon path is changed.

Compositions are measured from visible artwork bounds. The reference unit is the first E's capital height. This separates intended spacing from the invisible margins inside earlier SVG canvases. Small-size labels also refer to visible artwork width, unlike the canvas-width labels in round 15.

The minimum sizes and clear space are proposals for screen use, not validated print specifications. The 16px favicon remains unresolved. Founder confirmation of the weight, compositions and colour rules comes before a final export pack and application integration.
