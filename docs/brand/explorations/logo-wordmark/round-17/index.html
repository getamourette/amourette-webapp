One decision, limited to the favicon

Keep B1 everywhere if the original is clear enough. Choose F1 only if the extra weight helps at 16px without losing the ribbon's character. F2 tests how much can be removed before it becomes a different sign.

Framing, sources and what stays fixed

All three drawings retain a 512 × 512 frame with no individual recentering or enlargement. B1 occupies about 84% of the tile width. F2 leaves more space below because its ends are absent. A shared velvet background with 80-unit corners is a favicon proposal, not a new phone-icon specification.

B1 is fetched directly from the selected original. F1 and F2 are separate native SVG studies; neither overwrites it. The method notes explain the contour changes and PNG generation.

The core set remains B1 + More presence lettering, vertical main signature, horizontal companion, wordmark alone in app headers, and cream on velvet for the phone icon. General minimum sizes, clear space, other colour applications and final exports remain separate work. Nothing here is integrated into the app.
