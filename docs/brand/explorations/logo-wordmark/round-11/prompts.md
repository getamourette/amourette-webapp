# Conversation emblem studies — round 11

Mode: built-in image generation, one independent generation per concept.
These are new explorations, not a fusion or replacement of round 10 B and C.
Both existing marks and the round 9 wordmark remain unchanged.

Saved generated sources: `the-aside-source.png`, `the-reply-source.png`, and
`face-to-face-source.png`, in this directory. `build.mjs` fits their visible
contours with Potrace, accounting for alpha and excluding the generated glow.
The browser comparison uses flat `currentColor` SVGs; it does not modify the
preserved source bitmaps. Generated shapes remain exploration material, not
final hand-refined logo masters.

Each prompt consists of the shared brief followed by its concept brief below.

## Shared brief

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original companion symbol exploration for AMOURETTE, no lettering.
Brand context: Amourette brings people who are already in the same bar to a
discreet mutual match and a first conversation in person. Warm, assured,
intimate, elegant, slightly flirtatious. The existing wordmark is widely spaced
uppercase Cormorant Garamond with a custom gently sweeping R. The symbol should
harmonize with its sculpted thick-to-thin contrast. Beauty of the actual drawn
form matters as much as its association with conversation. This is NOT a
customer-support, AI-assistant, business-messaging, wedding, or gift brand.
Composition: one single large isolated symbol, centered on a square canvas,
occupying about 55 percent of the canvas width, with generous empty margins.
No duplicates, no board, no labels, no text, no wordmark.
Medium and palette: precise flat vector-like artwork, solid cream #EFE6E0 on
perfectly uniform near-black wine #120A0F. Balanced negative space, very few
intentional curves, refined optical balance. No texture, bloom, lighting,
shadows, gradients, bevels, 3D, or mockups. No hairline-only strokes. No heart,
flame, infinity sign, bow, knot, ribbon, star, or stock messaging icon.
Design a new symbol, not a remix of an existing ribbon emblem.

## D — The aside

Primary request: a beautifully drawn single conversational symbol, a bespoke
calligraphic speech form. One open-center, gently tilted oval enclosure with a
short expressive tapered tail, integrated into the enclosure rather than
attached as a triangle. The oval is sculpted: a full confident upper-left
stroke transitions into a finer lower-right return. A clean generous inner
counter. The tail curls outward subtly, like the ending of a spoken phrase.
It must still suggest speech, but feel closer to a custom typographic glyph
than a rounded-square chat-app icon. Near-square compact silhouette, elegant
asymmetry, minimal and warm. One enclosure and one short tail only; no inner
marks or dots, no extra flourishes, no uniform outline stroke, no literal
letter Q, no broken segments.

## E — The reply

Primary request: a pair of exquisitely sculpted quotation-like forms facing
each other, suggesting a voice and its reply. Each form has a lean curved
body with an airy open inner bowl and a short tapering terminal: graceful
calligraphic punctuation rather than plump solid comma blobs. Two separate
silhouettes with a visible central gap, subtly staggered vertically, almost
the same size. Give the pair a poised horizontal rhythm and beautiful
thick-to-thin transitions, like original engraved punctuation that belongs
beside a serif wordmark. Not a repeated standard font glyph. The forms should
feel fluid and confident, with concise sweeping endings, no dangling tails.
Do not join or weave them. No ribbon loops, no yin-yang, no leaves, no huge
bean-shaped bodies, no conventional C letters, no circular frame around them.

## F — Face to face / wildcard

Primary request: an unexpected poetic emblem for a first conversation in
person: two very reduced human side profiles facing one another across a
small, clearly visible shared space. Use the central negative space between
two confident curved silhouettes to reveal just the suggestion of a forehead,
nose, mouth and chin on each side. The people are not touching or kissing.
The two outer silhouettes together have an elegant compact horizontal oval
balance, without any enclosing outline. Keep the face contours extraordinarily
simple and smooth, almost an abstract typographic cameo: no eyes, hair,
eyelashes, gender coding, necks, bodies, portrait detail or line drawing.
The silhouettes have sculpted thick-to-thin curves that harmonize with a
serif wordmark. A quiet intimate exchange, not a medical or therapy symbol,
not a kiss illustration, not a yin-yang, not a literal pair of chat bubbles.
