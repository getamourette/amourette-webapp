Mode: built-in image generation, three independent reference-image edits,
followed by targeted edits for F1 and F2 and native vector work on F1.
Edit target: `../round-11/face-to-face-source.png`, inspected before editing.
Each edit uses the shared brief plus its specific brief below. Existing F,
B, C, and the round 9 wordmarks are preserved unchanged.

## Shared brief

Use case: logo-brand / precise-object-edit.
Input image 1: edit target, the current F emblem for Amourette.
Primary request: refine this emblem so its facing people become much less
literal. The viewer should first see a beautiful, poised abstract symbol;
two presences or an intimate exchange may be discovered afterwards. Improve
the actual contour drawing: fewer inflections, smooth controlled curves,
intentional tapered endings, beautiful negative space and optical balance.
Preserve the fundamental relationship of two separate inward-facing forms
and an open central space. Do not turn it into another subject or add objects.
Remove the explicit lips, mouth notches, pointed noses, chin shelves and
forehead/nose/lip/chin sequence in the source. Remove the horn-like top hooks.
No eyes, hair, portrait details, necks, gender cues, kissing or touching.
Brand: Amourette is an elegant, warm, discreet first-conversation app for
people already in the same bar. Its wordmark is widely spaced uppercase
Cormorant Garamond with a short sweeping R. Let the symbol share the serif
lettering's sculpted thick-to-thin contrast, without drawing any letters.
Output: one centered isolated emblem only, about 55 percent of a square
canvas's width. Solid flat cream #EFE6E0 on a completely uniform opaque
near-black wine #120A0F background. Absolutely no glow, light bloom, gradients,
shadows, textures, transparency effects, outlines, 3D, text or duplicates.
Avoid hearts, flames, bow/ribbon motifs, infinity symbols, yin-yang layouts,
obvious leaves, stock chat bubbles and generic beauty-clinic silhouettes.

## F1 — The hint

Stay closest to the current F's horizontal pair and visual weight, but replace
each facial edge with a single gentle, continuous contour with only one
subtle soft forward turn near its middle. That turn must not become a pointed
nose. The lower inner edge sweeps away smoothly, without any mouth notch or
chin. Two nearly balanced curved bodies, a little more compact and less
bulbous than the source. Smooth the outer contours into calm arcs and let
their upper and lower endings taper cleanly, without hooked horns. The
shared dark gap should be open, quiet, slightly waisted, not the outline of
two kissing faces. A faint face-to-face association is welcome but should
require a second look. Few masterful curves, subtle rather than ornate.

## F2 — The interval

Push the abstraction further and make the drawing more sculptural. Reduce
the two filled bodies into lean, elegant comma-crescent masses with controlled
thick-to-thin transitions. Their inner edges are smooth shallow S-like sweeps
with NO identifiable anatomical feature. Keep a generous, gently flowing
vertical interval between them. Give one form a very slight upward offset,
so they respond to one another without being a mechanically mirrored pair.
Together they form a compact softly horizontal emblem, more poised and
airy than the current F. On second look the shared space may suggest two
people, breath, or an exchange; none of those readings should be forced.
No interlocking, no swooping ribbon loops, no added stars or ornaments.

## F3 — Two breaths / wildcard

The deliberately more daring, distilled interpretation: remove most of the
filled bulk and reduce the face-to-face structure to exactly two sculpted
curved strokes, facing inward like an original pair of conversational
parentheses. Each has a confident thick outer belly that thins gracefully
toward its ends. No literal font punctuation: introduce a subtle asymmetry
in their sweep and tilt. The central empty space is the subject, generous
and calm; it might suggest a pause, a breath or two presences. No face
anatomy whatsoever. Near-square proportions, elegant sculptural contour
quality, clearly two separate elements and no enclosing circle. Not a pair
of leaves, not a yin-yang or an eye. A memorable quiet mark, not a hairline
ornament. Preserve the intimate two-part relationship, not the literal faces.

## F1 — Targeted refinement

Edit target: the first F1 generated study, not the original detailed faces.
Refine only this two-part emblem's contours. Keep two softly suggested
presences facing inward, cream #EFE6E0, dark #120A0F, the same horizontal
composition, and no additional elements. The current silhouettes are too
bulbous, the upper tips look like horns, and the bottom tips almost touch.
Make both bodies about 20 percent leaner, with more elegant curvature.
Replace each curled upper tip with a short smooth sloping termination,
without any upward hook. Open a clearly visible gap between the two bottom
ends. On the inner edge of each body, reduce the double-wave contour to
one very subtle broad forward turn that can be read as a profile only on
second look. No nose point, lips, mouth notch, chin shelf or facial details.
The negative space must feel open and calm, not a violin-shaped cavity.
Controlled long curves and graceful thick-to-thin transitions. Flat filled
shapes only, no glow, gradients, outlines, shadows, text or texture.

## F2 — Targeted refinement

Edit target: the first F2 generated study. Its two components feel too
disconnected and uneven. Keep a two-part abstract interpretation of a
face-to-face encounter, but align their top and bottom extents nearly
equally and bring their optical centers onto the same horizontal line.
Redraw them as two corresponding curved forms facing a generous central
vertical gap. Make both inner edges a long, very shallow, smoothly flowing
S-curve, with only a soft forward turn, never a recognizable nose or lips.
Round away the current hook-like bulbous terminals; taper the four ends
with concise graceful curves, not long spikes. Each body should flow from
a fine end into a firm middle and back to a fine end. Compact horizontal
balance, subtle rather than mechanical symmetry, sophisticated sculptural
contrast beside a serif wordmark. Preserve flat cream #EFE6E0 on uniform
dark #120A0F. No actual face details, no crescent moons, leaves, flame,
yin-yang, heart, enclosing frame, text, glow, texture or additional objects.
